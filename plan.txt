WHERE THINGS STAND
The phone test ran on a Google Pixel 8 Pro (Tensor G3, 11.3 GB RAM, CPU only, 4 threads, one run).
Results: Nabz/results/phone/google-pixel-8-pro.txt and the "Phone test" section of Nabz/results/RUN_NOTES.md.

  Speech (fine-tuned Whisper base + stock base witness, one after the other):
    24.2 s on the longest clip (37.6 s of audio). Budget 30 s. Fits, with a 6 s margin.
  Note writer (Qwen3.5 4B IQ3_M):
    3.98 tokens/s reading the prompt, 2.32 tokens/s writing.
    A long note = 400 / 3.98 + 360 / 2.32 = 101 s + 155 s = 256 s (4 min 16 s). Budget 30 s. Does not fit.
    Peak RAM 3.5 GB.

The Pixel 8 Pro is a flagship. The target is a mid-range phone with 6-8 GB RAM, which will be slower.
The note writer is the blocker for the Android app.

WHAT TO DO, IN THIS ORDER

1. Measure Qwen3.5 2B on the same phone.
   Why first: it is the fallback already scored on the PC (weaker notes: RUN_NOTES "Phone-tier note writers"),
   and it tells us whether any on-phone note writer is feasible.
   How: download the 2B GGUF (about 1.4 GB) into Download/nabz/ on the phone, point the llama-bench line of
   termux_bench.sh at it, rerun. The builds are already on the phone, so it takes a few minutes.
   Record: prompt and generation tokens/s, seconds per note by the same formula, peak RAM.
   Save as Nabz/results/phone/google-pixel-8-pro-qwen35-2b.txt and add it to RUN_NOTES.

2. Decide on the note writer from that number.
   a. If 2B fits 30 s (or is close): compare its note quality against 4B IQ3_M on the test speakers from the
      existing PC runs, and decide whether the quality loss is acceptable.
   b. If 2B is also too slow: accept a slow note. The transcript appears in about 25 s; the note is written in
      the background and arrives minutes later. This changes the app design, not the models.
   c. Optional, only if a and b are both unacceptable: try llama.cpp's GPU back end on Android. Untested here and
      may not work on the Tensor chip.

3. Run the same benchmark on a mid-range 6-8 GB phone, if one is available.
   The speech margin (6 s on a flagship) may not hold there. Steps: Nabz/phone/README.md.
